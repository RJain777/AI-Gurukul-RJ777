Certification path

Anthropic's Claude Certification Program runs from a foundations credential to a professional architect credential. Each card shows your readiness on the terms this atlas maps to it.

Who can sit the exams

Exams are proctored, identity-verified and delivered through Pearson, with digital badges via Credly. They are available to members of the Claude Partner Network — if your organisation is a member, ask your learning team about the route and any exam voucher.

Where to prepare

Preparation courses for each credential sit in Anthropic Partner Academy. Free public courses on Claude Academy (academy.claude.com) — AI Fluency, Claude 101, Building with the Claude API, Introduction to MCP, Claude Code in Action — cover the same ground.

How to use this page

Start at Fundamentals even if you're technical: it builds shared vocabulary. Then take Architect: Foundations, since your work is designing systems rather than only coding them. Details change often; check the official exam guide before booking.

Why this order

Vocabulary first, because it's what lets you lead meetings. Building second, because it turns vocabulary into judgement. Certification third, because it's far easier once you've built the things it asks about. Governance and leadership last, because they need all three.
